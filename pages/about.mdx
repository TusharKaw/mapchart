# 🌍 About MapChart

Welcome to **MapChart**, the simplest and most flexible way to create custom maps online.  
Whether you’re a student, teacher, researcher, or data enthusiast, MapChart helps you turn geographic data into **clear, beautiful visualizations**.

---

## ✨ Why MapChart?

- 🗺️ **Customizable Maps** – Choose from world maps, continents, regions, and country-level breakdowns.  
- 🎨 **Easy Styling** – Fill areas with colors, add labels, and highlight territories.  
- 📊 **Data-Driven** – Perfect for demographics, statistics, or historical data.  
- ⚡ **Instant Export** – Download high-quality images for presentations, reports, or social media.
